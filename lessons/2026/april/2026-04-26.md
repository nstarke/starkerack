# April 26 - Performance Macros

## Focus
Create a few hands-on controls that reshape the whole patch.

## Modules
Pam's Pro Workout, Batumi, Vector Space, MISO, VC8, Levita8, Keystep Pro, Expert Sleepers ES-9

## Manuals To Skim
Pam's Pro Workout, Batumi, Vector Space, MISO, VC8, Levita8, Keystep Pro, ES-9

## 60-Minute Plan
1. `0:00-0:10` Pick three global controls: tension, density, and space.
2. `0:10-0:25` Build macro control paths with attenuverters, mixers, VCAs, and CV routing.
3. `0:25-0:40` Assign one performance gesture to each macro.
4. `0:40-0:55` Practice moving only those three controls through a full section change.
5. `0:55-1:00` Note which macro genuinely helps performance and which just moves too many things at once.

## Questions
- Which three macro ideas matter most to your music?
- Are the macro moves reversible and predictable?
- What needs direct manual control instead of a macro?
- Did any macro flatten the patch by changing too much?
- Which control would you want under your hand in a live set?
