# April 11 - Build a Drum Ecosystem

## Focus
Combine everything from the first ten days into a drum system with hierarchy.

## Modules
Pam's Pro Workout, Grids, Marbles, your preferred divider, your preferred kick, your preferred snare, one metallic voice, Erica Drum Mixer, MISO

## Manuals To Skim
Revisit the manuals from days 1 through 10 that gave you the best results

## 60-Minute Plan
1. `0:00-0:10` Choose one master source for structure, one for variation, and one for timbre movement.
2. `0:10-0:25` Patch kick, snare, hats, and one strange percussion line.
3. `0:25-0:40` Use utilities to attenuate every modulation source before it hits a destination.
4. `0:40-0:55` Remove one unnecessary voice and one unnecessary modulation path.
5. `0:55-1:00` Write a short note on the difference between "many events" and "clear structure."

## Questions
- Which layer is the rhythmic anchor?
- Which layer creates unease?
- What did you remove that improved the patch?
- Which utility module quietly solved the biggest problem?
- Can you hear a hierarchy of foreground, midground, and background rhythm?
