# April 15 - Slow Motion Modulation

## Focus
Make pads evolve over minutes instead of seconds.

## Modules
Xaoc Batumi, Erica Synths Octasource, Make Noise MultiMod, Worng Vector Space, Buchla and Tiptop Audio 281t, Doepfer A-143-1, MISO

## Manuals To Skim
Batumi, Octasource, MultiMod, Vector Space, 281t, A-143-1, MISO

## 60-Minute Plan
1. `0:00-0:10` Pick one pad voice from days 12 through 14.
2. `0:10-0:25` Send one slow modulation to filter cutoff and a different slow modulation to oscillator timbre.
3. `0:25-0:40` Use Vector Space or MISO to combine or offset modulation sources.
4. `0:40-0:55` Listen for interactions over at least two minutes without touching the patch.
5. `0:55-1:00` Identify the one modulation path that genuinely creates long-form development.

## Questions
- Which modulation source feels most organic over time?
- Are multiple slow modulations supporting each other or fighting?
- What changes are audible only after a minute?
- Where does attenuation matter most?
- Does the pad still feel ominous when it moves very slowly?
