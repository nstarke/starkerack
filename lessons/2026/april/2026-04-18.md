# April 18 - Granular Fog I

## Focus
Turn pads into weather systems.

## Modules
Mutable Instruments Clouds, Qu-Bit Mojave, Make Noise Mimeophon, Erica Synths Stereo Reverb, Earthquaker Devices Afterneath

## Manuals To Skim
Clouds, Mojave, Mimeophon, Stereo Reverb, Afterneath

## 60-Minute Plan
1. `0:00-0:10` Feed yesterday's pad or drone into Clouds or Mojave.
2. `0:10-0:25` Find one frozen or semi-frozen texture that still preserves pitch identity.
3. `0:25-0:40` Modulate position, grain size, density, or zone slowly.
4. `0:40-0:55` Compare granular texture into delay versus delay into granular texture.
5. `0:55-1:00` Record the most convincing "ominous fog" and note the exact buffer behavior.

## Questions
- Which processor preserves mood while increasing scale?
- Did freezing the sound make it more cinematic or more static?
- Which parameter gave the most useful motion?
- Does the texture still imply harmony?
- What made the result eerie rather than pretty?
