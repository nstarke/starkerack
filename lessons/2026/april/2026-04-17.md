# April 17 - Subharmonics and Labyrinths

## Focus
Use semi-self-running voices to generate pads and drones with built-in tension.

## Modules
Moog Subharmonicon, Moog Labyrinth, Moog Mother-32, Moog Mavis, Korg Volca FM2, Korg Volca Bass

## Manuals To Skim
Subharmonicon, Labyrinth, Mother-32, Mavis, Volca FM2, Volca Bass

## 60-Minute Plan
1. `0:00-0:10` Pick one semi-autonomous instrument as the pitch source.
2. `0:10-0:25` Build a drone or repeating harmonic figure with minimal external control.
3. `0:25-0:40` Feed it into your preferred filter and space effect chain.
4. `0:40-0:55` Lock one voice to the drum pulse while letting another drift against it.
5. `0:55-1:00` Decide whether the most ominous result came from strict tuning or slight instability.

## Questions
- Which instrument creates tension with the least patching?
- Do subharmonics sound mournful, threatening, or simply dense?
- What amount of drift still feels musical?
- Which voice sits best behind complex drums?
- Did the autonomous behavior help composition or make it harder to focus?
