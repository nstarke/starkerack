# April 10 - Digital Drum Character

## Focus
Use digital drum voices for variation, not for maximum density.

## Modules
Erica Synths LXR Eurorack, ALM Dinky's Taiko, Noise Engineering Basimilus Iteritas Alia, Intellijel Plonk, WMD SSF Facture, Erica Synths Drum Sequencer

## Manuals To Skim
LXR Eurorack, Dinky's Taiko, Basimilus Iteritas Alia, Plonk, Facture

## 60-Minute Plan
1. `0:00-0:10` Pick one digital voice for kick-adjacent duty and one for metallic or noise duty.
2. `0:10-0:25` Sequence a restrained pattern and explore accents, not just raw parameter extremes.
3. `0:25-0:40` Use modulation to move one macro parameter slowly over several bars.
4. `0:40-0:55` Layer one digital voice with one analog voice from earlier lessons.
5. `0:55-1:00` Decide which digital voice adds identity and which just adds brightness.

## Questions
- Which digital voice feels most playable?
- What parameter changes sound musical across a whole phrase?
- Does layering make the hit richer or blurrier?
- Which voice can stay interesting with the least modulation?
- What kind of "machine-ness" do you actually want in your music?
