# April 13 - Alternate Pad Voices

## Focus
Learn which of your voice modules can become pads even if they are not marketed that way.

## Modules
Mutable Instruments Plaits, Mutable Instruments Braids, Intellijel Atlantix, Pittsburgh Modular Taiga Desktop, Erica Synths Graphic VCO, Industrial Music Electronics Piston Honda

## Manuals To Skim
Plaits, Braids, Atlantix, Taiga Desktop, Graphic VCO, Piston Honda

## 60-Minute Plan
1. `0:00-0:10` Pick three voice modules and find their softest sustained territory.
2. `0:10-0:25` Build a pad from the least obvious candidate.
3. `0:25-0:40` Compare a stable harmonic pad against a more unstable digital one.
4. `0:40-0:55` Layer two pad voices quietly and remove whichever one does less.
5. `0:55-1:00` Note which voice feels best for lead pad duty and which works best as a ghost layer.

## Questions
- Which module surprised you by working as a pad?
- What kind of instability sounds eerie instead of harsh?
- Does layering help because of spectrum, motion, or width?
- Which pad voice would still work in a very dry mix?
- What makes one digital oscillator feel cinematic and another feel merely noisy?
