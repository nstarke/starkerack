# April 20 - Distortion as Atmosphere

## Focus
Use saturation and destruction to darken both drums and pads without flattening everything.

## Modules
Schlappi 100 Grit, Bastl Dark Matter, Erica Synths and Gamechanger Plasma Drive, Noise Engineering Sec Ruina, Noise Engineering Terci Ruina, ADDAC-714 Vintage Clipper, Recovery Effects Bad Comrade

## Manuals To Skim
100 Grit, Dark Matter, Plasma Drive, Sec Ruina, Terci Ruina, ADDAC-714, Bad Comrade

## 60-Minute Plan
1. `0:00-0:10` Patch one drum bus and one pad bus dry.
2. `0:10-0:25` Distort the drum bus first, aiming for sharper contour rather than loudness.
3. `0:25-0:40` Distort the pad bus differently, aiming for dread, grain, or unstable edges.
4. `0:40-0:55` Compare insert distortion, parallel distortion, and modulation of the distortion amount.
5. `0:55-1:00` Keep one distortion move and remove the rest.

## Questions
- Which distortion deepens the mood instead of just adding aggression?
- Does the pad need harmonic damage or dynamic instability?
- Which bus benefits more from parallel treatment?
- What disappears first when you distort too much?
- How can distortion increase separation instead of reducing it?
